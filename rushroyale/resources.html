RUSH ROYALE · RESOURCES

Dust,
orbs & gold

Rush Royale has a lot of currencies and they are easy to mix up. Each one below answers the same three questions: what it is, where it comes from, what to spend it on.

Last reviewed 2026-08-30 · Helped with Claude Code

The short version

Twenty-four things accumulate in your account. Nearly every mix-up is between two items inside the same group, so it helps to see them grouped.

And one that is never spent

Trophies are a rating rather than a currency: they decide your arena, and therefore what you face.

Chests are not on this list. A chest is not something you collect — it is the container that hands you the things above, and which ones depend on the type of chest. The Lucky Casket, for instance, is the chest bonus orbs come out of.

Magic dust vs magic orbs

These two get confused constantly because both have to do with legendaries. They do opposite jobs:

Magic DustMagic Orbs
Gets youA legendary card you do not haveA level on a legendary you already have
Rate1,000 dust → one random legendarySpent per upgrade, at every level
CapNone — accumulate freelyWhatever you can earn
Feels likeA slow guaranteeA wall

In short: dust gets legendaries into your collection, orbs make them usable. Collecting legendaries you have no orbs to level is the most common way to feel stuck.

There is a third thing with orb in the name. Bonus orbs are magic orbs bound to one named unit: a bonus orb for Bard knocks one magic orb off the price of a Bard level and does nothing at all for anybody else. Levelling an event unit with ordinary orbs is what produces them, and they then settle on legendaries of the same faction.

The Laboratory

Several of these resources have nowhere to go but the Laboratory, and it is not one counter — it is a set of rooms, each taking something different:

RoomYou put inYou get back
Legendary Lab1,000 magic dustA legendary card
Magic LabLegendary cardsA magic orb
Laboratory of Experiments30 experiment coinsOne experiment — see below
Glutton MonstersA legendary cardA magic orb, or another card — with a possibility of change

From league 1 onwards the Glutton exchange can hand back event cards as well.

How the experiments work

This is the closest the game comes to letting you pick which card you get. You nominate five cards you already own, and each experiment has a chance of handing one of those five back at random. One of the five slots can be a magic orb rather than a unit, so four units and an orb is a normal shape to run.

The odds climb with each experiment in the series:

ExperimentChance of a card
1st10%
2nd20%
3rd30%
4th40%
5thGuaranteed

Each experiment costs 30 coins, so five of them is 150 — that is the real price of the guarantee, and it is worth thinking of it that way rather than gambling on the 10% and being surprised. Whichever card it gives you is still drawn at random from your five, so the choice you actually make is which five you nominate.

A unit that has just been released cannot be nominated. New units become selectable later, so experiments are not a route to the card that launched this week.

What to spend on first

  • Gold on the cards you actually play. A levelled Common in your deck beats an unlevelled Legendary sitting in your collection.
  • Orbs on two or three legendaries, not eight. Orbs are the bottleneck, so spreading them thin leaves you with several legendaries that are all too weak to bring.
  • Crystals on chests. Cards are the scarce thing; chests are how cards arrive.
  • Rune keys on the Chest of Magic Dust, if you are working towards a legendary you cannot otherwise get.

Three traps

Hoarding dust for no reason

Dust is uncapped and does not expire, so there is no penalty for holding it — but also no benefit past 1,000. If you are at the threshold, trade it.

Levelling cards you do not play

Gold spent on a card that never makes your five is gold that did nothing. Decide the deck first, then feed it.

Letting event tokens hit the ceiling

Event tokens do not expire when an event ends, which makes holding them feel safe — but the balance stops at 600, and everything earned above that is gone. Spend down before an event you expect to do well in, not after it.
