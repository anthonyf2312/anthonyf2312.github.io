What items are

Items are equipment you attach to a hero, and they raise stats across your whole account rather than for one card. They cover a wide spread — from how much damage you deal to how much mana you get for killing something.

Because they apply to everything, items are the least visible and most reliable power in the game. Nothing about them changes what you do during a match; they change what your board is worth when you get there.

Slots

A hero has between two and four item slots, and unlocks more of them as it levels. Each slot takes one item.

This is a second reason to level heroes, on top of the hero itself: every extra slot is an entire extra item's worth of stats. See heroes.

Item rarity

Items use the same four tiers as cards — Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary — but here rarity does more work. A rarer item is straightforwardly stronger, and it adds power aimed at specific factions rather than at everything.

So two items with the same name are not the same item. A faction bonus is also only worth something if your deck actually contains units from that faction — otherwise it is a dead line of text.

Fragments and crafting

Every item is crafted, and the recipe is the same shape each time:

  • 200 fragments of the specific slot you want to fill, plus
  • some gold to finish the craft.

Fragments are slot-specific — amulet fragments only ever become amulets — so you cannot pool them into whichever item you want most. They come from general play and from Career quest rewards, among other places. See item fragments.

Choosing what to equip

Straight damage is never wrong and rarely the best use of a slot. The stats that move the needle are the ones your deck is already built to exploit:

  • Running crit units like Rogue or Sharpshooter? Critical damage compounds with what they already do.
  • Running fast, light hitters like Wind Archer, or a ramping unit like Crystalmancer? Attack speed does more than raw damage.
  • Constantly out of mana mid-match? Mana from kills fixes the actual problem, which is not that your units are too weak.
