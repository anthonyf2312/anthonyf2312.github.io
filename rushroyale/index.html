Everything the game explains badly, or not at all — written for someone who has never opened it. No jargon without a definition, no numbers that might be wrong.

Unofficial community reference. Not affiliated with My.Games or the Rush Royale team.

Rules confirmed by someone who plays the game are stated plainly, numbers and all. Entries marked with a hatched edge, or with a Needs numbers line, are still waiting on figures checked in-game.

The rules pages now follow the game's in-game Knowledge Base, recorded 2026-08-27. That covers how the Phantom and bosses work in PvP, co-op's chapters and floors, heroes and ability points, legendarity, spells and artifacts, profile perks, reincarnation and celestial shards.

The unit roster still comes from the Rush Royale Fandom wiki, and that wiki is old. Roughly three quarters of its pages were last edited in 2021 or 2022. Imported material is labelled as such and carries the source page's revision date, so you can tell it apart from what came from the game itself. Where the two disagreed — notably how bosses arrive in PvP — the in-game text wins and the wiki's version has been removed rather than left standing beside it.
