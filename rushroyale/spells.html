Spells, artifacts
& perks

A second axis of deckbuilding that sits beside your five cards — and a set of permanent bonuses that sit behind all of them.

This page replaces the old items page. Heroes no longer have item slots and there is no equipment to craft for them; a hero's own power now comes from ability points. What you equip to a deck is what this page covers.

Spells and artifacts

Spells and artifacts are unlocked by gaining legendarity. They let you make a deck more powerful, or build new battle tactics that the five cards alone could not.

You take a fixed loadout into battle:

  • One spell.
  • Up to three artifacts.

Because legendarity is what unlocks them, this is the payoff for upgrading legendary units and heroes rather than spreading investment thin. The higher your legendarity, the more choices you have here.

Relics

Among the artifacts you will find relics. Only one copy of each relic can exist in a single deck.

That is the whole rule, and it is the only place the artifact list has a deckbuilding constraint attached to it.

Artifact slots

Artifact slots are unlocked when you first reach a required amount of trophies. Once unlocked they stay unlocked in subsequent seasons — a season reset does not take them back.

The in-game text does not state the trophy thresholds, so they are not listed here. This is a gap worth filling from the game.

Upgrading

A random unlocked artifact can be upgraded for essence. You do not choose which one the upgrade lands on.

The six archetypes

Each ability has one of six archetypes, and the archetype tells you what kind of effect it has:

ArchetypeWhat it specialises in
DamageIncreasing the damage you deal.
EconomyGranting extra mana, merge ranks or units.
MonstersMaking the opponent's monsters more powerful.
NegativesWeakening the opponent in various ways.
EmpowermentMaking decks suited for special strategies stronger.
DefenseProtecting your units against various negative effects.

Two of these read oddly until you remember the Phantom: Monsters strengthens the opponent's monsters and Negatives weakens the opponent directly, both of which are ways of attacking a player you never touch.

Profile perks

Profile perks are separate from anything you equip. They give a permanent bonus — either to one of the stats of the units on the field, or to your results in various game modes.

They are unlocked as the critical damage of your profile's collection grows, so they follow the same investment that critical damage does.

To develop a perk you need perk fragments and gold.

Perks are the quietest power in the game: nothing about them changes what you do during a match, only what your board is worth when you get there. See perk fragments.
